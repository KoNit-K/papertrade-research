## Teaching checks

Independent floating-point arithmetic checked the documented holdback endpoints, an interior point of each curve, and a historical BTC launch-parameter example. At 10 bps favorable movement and $100,000 notional, the reduced gain is $98, scaled gain about $73.91315; the normal fee branch leaves about $72.43488 without builder or $72.06532 with builder. These are profit amounts before adding returned margin. They are illustrative calculations, not Solidity rounding or a production settlement replay. The mint example in the product review starts from an already-eligible basis and does not assume a complete loss-fee ledger.
